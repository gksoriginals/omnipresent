# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, explain disagreements, and respect people's privacy and time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation, doxxing and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, PRs, discussions and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban participants when needed. Reports are considered in context and handled with discretion; enforcement decisions may be appealed to the maintainer.

For non-sensitive concerns, ask a maintainer in the relevant thread. For private conduct reports, use contact information on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/gksoriginals). If no suitable private route is listed, open a content-free issue requesting private contact; do not publish personal details or allegations. Security vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md instead.
