# ADR-008: Synthetic voice test tooling

Recorded: 17 October 2026 (retrospective). Status: **Test-only adoption**.

## Context and alternatives

Three voice variants were exercised. Earlier say-based tests remain separate. No comparative TTS quality study exists.

## Decision

Piper generates repeatable narrative variants for actual STT replays.

## Evidence

EXP-006, EXP-011–012. See the [register](../validation/test-register.md) and [experiment reports](../validation/experiment-reports.md) for methods, failures and limitations. Versioned package/runtime locks and the software audit identify the current implementation.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

External engine/voice metadata and hashes are retained. This is evaluation tooling, not a product TTS choice.
