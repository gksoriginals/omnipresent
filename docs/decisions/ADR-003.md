# ADR-003: Streaming speech recognition

Recorded: 17 October 2026 (retrospective). Status: **Provisional**.

## Context and alternatives

Actual streamed WAV and cancellation measurements exist; Medium, sherpa and competing engines lack controlled trials.

## Decision

Local Moonshine Small in a Python worker.

## Evidence

EXP-001, EXP-006, EXP-011–012. See the [register](../validation/test-register.md) and [experiment reports](../validation/experiment-reports.md) for methods, failures and limitations. Versioned package/runtime locks and the software audit identify the current implementation.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

Need physical microphone, noise, language and contention evaluations before choosing a production STT engine.
