# ADR-009: Live System One decision models and controllers

Recorded: 17 October 2026 (retrospective). Status: **Prototype; selection pending**.

## Context and alternatives

Jev, Microsoft, local Laya and hosted Kev have development-screen evidence. Initial offline rules/FSM/BT/hybrid comparison is completed as EXP-022; initial model-assisted semantic shortlist selection is measured in EXP-024/025; independent labels and asynchronous integration remain planned.

## Decision

Keep typed decision adapters and compare deterministic candidate controllers before selecting a production decision path.

## Evidence

EXP-015–019, EXP-022–026, PLAN-001. See the [register](../validation/test-register.md) and [experiment reports](../validation/experiment-reports.md) for methods, failures and limitations. Versioned package/runtime locks and the software audit identify the current implementation.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

No quality winner. Default app adapter has not yet migrated from the general local model. Use the small FSM plus explicit guards as the provisional next baseline; see [ADR-012](ADR-012.md). Improve candidate retrieval and validate independently before integrating the selected path.
