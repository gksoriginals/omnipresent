# ADR-005: Preparation language model

Recorded: 17 October 2026 (retrospective). Status: **Provisional**.

## Context and alternatives

Actual interview/story/visual calls work. Larger models, Groq and alternative runtimes have not been compared. Live decision trials are a different workload.

## Decision

Self-hosted Qwen3.5:4b via Ollama for preparation.

## Evidence

EXP-003–005, EXP-017–018. See the [register](../validation/test-register.md) and [experiment reports](../validation/experiment-reports.md) for methods, failures and limitations. Versioned package/runtime locks and the software audit identify the current implementation.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

Do not generalize one tool-schema failure to all LLMs. Revisit on preparation quality, tool reliability and measured machine contention.
